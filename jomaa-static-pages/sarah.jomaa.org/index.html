    About

    Sarah makes paintings about light, weather and the shapes that are left when detail falls away. She works mostly in acrylic and gouache, with a growing body of screen prints.

    Her studies start as small sketches made outdoors and are finished in the studio, where colour is pushed further than the scene ever was.
